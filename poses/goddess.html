How to improve your Goddess Pose

Wide stance, toes turned out, hips low, knees over toes, torso tall, arms in a goalpost. Here’s the rubric Posewise measures it against, and what to train when you miss.

What’s holding it back

Build leg strength and open your inner thighs, and you’ll sink deeper into the squat.

Depth usually stops where leg strength runs out. Tight inner thighs can cap it too.

Quick answers

How low should I squat?

Aim for thighs close to parallel with the floor, but only as low as your knees can stay over your toes. A higher squat with good knees beats a deep one with knees caving in.

How far should my toes turn out?

About 45°, with your heels in and toes out. Your knees should point the same direction as your toes.

Why do my knees cave in during Goddess Pose?

Usually weak outer hips, or toes turned out further than your hips allow. Press your knees out toward your little toes.

What do I do with my arms?

The common version is cactus arms: elbows at shoulder height, bent to about 90°, palms forward. Hands at your heart works too.

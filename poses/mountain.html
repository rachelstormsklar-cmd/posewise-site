How to improve your Mountain Pose

Standing tall, shoulders over hips, ears over shoulders, weight centered through both feet. Here’s the rubric Posewise measures it against, and what to train when you miss.

The rubric

Posewise checks three things from the side. Here’s what good looks like.

  • TorsoUpright, shoulders stacked over hips0–10°lean from vertical
  • HeadEars over your shouldersStackedear vs shoulder
  • NeckLong, crown lifting0–14°neck from vertical

What’s holding it back

Build core strength, and you’ll stand tall, shoulders stacked over your hips.

A lean usually means the trunk isn’t holding itself up, or your weight has drifted into your heels or toes.

Quick answers

Should my feet be together?

Either way works. Big toes touching is traditional; hip-width apart is steadier if you wobble.

Where should my weight be?

Spread evenly across both feet, between the heels and the balls of the feet, not sinking back into your heels.

Should I tuck my tailbone?

Not hard. Keep the natural curve in your low back with your pelvis level, and let your ribs sit over your hips.

What do I do with my arms?

Let them hang relaxed by your sides, palms in or facing forward, with your shoulders soft and away from your ears.

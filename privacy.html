Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026 · Applies to the Posewise app

Hawthorn West, Inc. ("we," "us") makes the Posewise app, which uses your phone's camera to check your yoga pose alignment and build a practice plan. This policy explains what information the app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

The short version: Your camera feed is analyzed on your phone and never uploaded. If you create an account, we store your practice history so it isn't lost across devices or reinstalls. We use product analytics to understand how the app is used, and you can turn it off anytime. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps.

Information we collect

Camera

Posewise needs camera access to analyze your pose. Video frames are processed on your device by a pose-detection model (Google MediaPipe) that runs locally. Camera images and video are never sent to us or anyone else.

Pose and practice data

From each assessment, the app saves:

  • Body position points: the estimated positions of 33 points on your body (such as shoulders, hips, knees, and a few facial points like the eyes and nose). These are coordinates, not images.
  • Alignment results: the measurements and scores calculated from those points.
  • Practice activity: your training plans, completed sessions and their dates, weekly goal, streaks, and app settings such as voice coaching.

This data is stored on your device and backed up to our server under your account (see below). We use it only to run the app: to show your progress, compare check-ins, and build your plan. We don't use body position points to identify you.

Account information

If you create an account, we collect:

  • Email and password: your password is handled by our authentication provider and is never stored in plain text; or
  • Sign in with Apple: your email address (or the private relay address Apple provides if you choose to hide it) and, the first time you sign in, the name you share with Apple.

Usage analytics

To understand how people use Posewise and improve it, the app records events such as signing in, starting and finishing an assessment or practice session, which pose you practiced, and changes to settings like your weekly goal. Each event includes basic device information (such as device model, iOS version, app version, and language) and is linked to your account by a random user ID, never your name or email.

Analytics never include camera images, body position points, or alignment results. We don't collect your IP address, city, or mobile carrier for analytics.

If you're in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, analytics are off until you agree to them. Everywhere else they're on by default. Anyone can turn them off in Settings → Share Usage Data.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising or cross-app tracking software
  • No precise location, contacts, photos library, microphone recordings, or Apple Health data
  • No tracking of your activity across other companies' apps or websites

How your data is stored and shared

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We use these service providers to run the app:

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and synced data until you delete your account. You can delete your account and all associated data anytime in the app under Settings → Delete Account. Deleting your account permanently removes your cloud data and asks Amplitude to delete your analytics data, which it completes within 30 days. You can also email us at hello@hawthornwest.com to request deletion.

Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Analytics: Turn usage analytics on or off anytime in Settings → Share Usage Data.
  • Camera: You can turn camera access on or off anytime in iOS Settings → Posewise. The app can't analyze your pose without it.
  • Access, correction, or deletion: Contact us at hello@hawthornwest.com. Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, UK, or California), you may have additional rights, such as getting a copy of your data or objecting to certain uses of it. We'll honor these requests as the law requires.

If you're in the European Economic Area (EEA) or UK

Hawthorn West, Inc. is the controller of your personal information.

Legal basis. We process your information to provide the app and your account at your request (performance of a contract), and to keep the service secure and working (our legitimate interests). We use analytics only with your consent, which you can withdraw anytime in Settings.

International transfers. Your account, synced data, and analytics data are stored on servers in the United States. When we transfer data from the EEA or UK to the US, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK equivalent, in our agreements with service providers.

Your rights. You can ask us to give you access to your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or send you a copy in a portable format. Email hello@hawthornwest.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Posewise is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK), and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here and change the "Last updated" date. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we'll notify you in the app.
